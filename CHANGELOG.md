# Changelog

## v1.0

First named public release, byte-identical to the tested Prototype 21.

- Select Terry on Ryu's slot with Select; original Ryu remains available.
- NGPC-derived combat artwork, portraits, HUD face/name and roster icon.
- Backspin Kick, Power Wave, Rising Tackle, Crack Shoot, Burn Knuckle,
  Power Dunk, Fire Kick and Power Geyser adaptations.
- Directional punch throws, Alpha Counter, crouching diagonal-kick correction.
- Corrected rolling recovery: four centred tumbling frames replace the
  misassigned upright jumping poses.
- Includes the existing Akuma, Dan and M. Bison selector changes.
- Expanded 4 MiB cartridge; standard GBC ROM operation, no custom firmware needed.

See README.md for inputs, exact base-ROM requirements and known limitations.
